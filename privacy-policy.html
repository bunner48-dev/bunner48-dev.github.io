Elisea: Coral Adventure — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Elisea: Coral Adventure (the "Game") handles information. The Game is designed for everyone, including children.

The Game itself does not collect personal information

The Game has no accounts, login, chat, location, camera or microphone features. It never asks players for a name, email address, phone number, photo or any other personal information.

Game progress (best score, coins and gems, wardrobe choices, sound and language settings) is stored only on the device itself, is never sent to any server, and is removed when the app is uninstalled.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are shown only when the player taps a "Watch ad" button (rewarded ads).

  • All ad requests are tagged as child-directed (COPPA "child-directed treatment").
  • Only ads rated for general audiences (G) are requested.
  • No personalized ads are used; there is no interest-based targeting.
  • The Game does not have permission to use the Android Advertising ID (AAID) or Privacy Sandbox ad services.

To show and count ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as IP address, device model and operating system version. See Google's advertising policies and AdMob and children's privacy.

Permissions

Internet: to load ads. The Game can also be played offline.
Vibration: for in-game haptic feedback; it can be turned off in Settings.

For parents

The Game has no in-app purchases. Ads are optional and the Game can be played without watching any ads. For questions or requests, please contact us at the address below.
